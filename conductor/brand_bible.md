# BRAND BIBLE: FAITH POINTS (Sovereign Edition)
**Version:** 1.0 (Sacred Realism Noir)
**Objective:** High-retention strategic analysis of biblical narratives for a global English-speaking audience.

---

## 1. THE CORE IDENTITY: SACRED REALISM NOIR
The channel rejects traditional "sugary" religious aesthetics. Instead, it positions biblical texts as ancient intelligence protocols and military strategy manuals.

### Visual Pillars:
- **Gravity:** Every frame must feel heavy, historic, and consequential.
- **Chiaroscuro:** High contrast. Deep blacks representing the darkness of the world vs. warm amber light representing divine truth.
- **Textural Integrity:** Focus on the "grain" of reality — worn leather, scratched iron, tattered paper, flickering candles.

---

## 2. THE VISUAL PROTOCOL (UI/UX)

### A. The Fragment Protocol (The Irregular Mask)
Instead of full-screen overlays, use a **distressed border / torn paper mask**.
- **Visual Effect:** A central rectangular mask with irregular, jagged, or burnt edges.
- **Function:** It creates the illusion of looking at a "Classified Document" or a "Recovered Scroll."
- **Behavior:** The mask should have a subtle "breathing" motion (slow zoom-in/out).

### B. Color Palette (The Sovereign Spectrum)
- **Background:** Absolute Black (#000000) or Slate Noir (#1A1A1A).
- **Primary Accent:** Sovereign Gold (#D4AF37) — Used for Macro Concepts and highlights.
- **Theological Glow:** Warm Amber (#FFBF00) — Used for candlelight and divine focus points.
- **Text Body:** Ivory Parchment (#F5F5DC) — For Bible verses inside the fragment.

### C. Typography
- **Bible Verses:** Classic Serif (e.g., *Cinzel* or *EB Garamond*), Italicized, Ivory color.
- **Macro Concepts:** Heavy Sans-Serif (e.g., *Montserrat Bold* or *Bebas Neue*), Brushed Gold texture, All Caps.
- **Typeface behavior:** Use a heavy "Mechanical Typewriter" or "Chiseled Stone" appearance effect.

---

## 3. THE AUDITORY ENGINE

### A. The Narrator's Voice
- **Tone:** Pragmatic, deep, and authoritative.
- **Style:** Close-mic recording (proximity effect). The narrator is an "Architect" or a "General" delivering a briefing, not a preacher.

### B. Soundscape (Drones & Percussion)
- **Base:** Low-frequency cinematic drones (C2-C3 range).
- **Percussion:** Distant, heavy war drums (Taiko style) used sparingly to punctuate macro concepts.
- **Foley:** Textural sounds — scraping metal, flickering fire, heavy breathing, or the sound of an ink pen on parchment.

---

## 4. EDITING DIRECTIVES (RETENTION MAXIMIZATION)

1. **The 3-Second Rule:** Something must subtly change every 3 seconds (Zoom, Pan, light flicker, or text reveal).
2. **The Macro-Pop:** When a strategic concept appears (outside the fragment mask), use a subtle "bass hit" or "metal clang" sound.
3. **Poda Coercitiva (Coercive Pruning):** Never flood the screen with text. One verse inside the fragment, one or two macro words outside.

---

## 5. CONTENT ARCHITECTURE (5-MINUTE FLOW)
- **0:00 - 0:15 (The Hook):** Subversive truth that breaks a common religious myth.
- **0:15 - 1:30 (The Archive):** Historical/Biblical context presented as a "Case Study."
- **1:30 - 3:30 (The Strategic Analysis):** Connecting the text to modern human nature/survival.
- **3:30 - 4:45 (The Counter-Intelligence):** The solution (The Faith Point) based on logic and virtue.
- **4:45 - 5:00 (The Order):** A direct command to the viewer. No "Like & Subscribe" begging.
